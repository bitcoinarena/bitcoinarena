  • Budget per week: 150,000 sats - a pot of 100,000 sats for bugs, improvements, community bonuses and node bounties, and a draw of 50,000 sats at the end of the week. When the pot is empty, new reports wait for the next week.

Week 2 ends 2026-11-21 07:12 UTC; the seed is the hash of the first Bitcoin block mined after that time (the first block on Bitcoin mainnet whose header time is after the end; a block stamped exactly at the end does not count). How to report: play a round, then use Feedback at the bottom of the game page. No sign-up and no Nostr key needed.

Our open-issues list is kept short on purpose: it names the area, not the bug. Whether a report is new, a duplicate of a known issue, or a follow-up, and which level it gets, is decided by Bitcoin Arena. Our decision is final. If a report is a duplicate, we tell you the ticket number, so you can check it once the fix is public.

Week 1 (2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13): rules and payouts

Week 1 ran from 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-13. Every payment is listed below.

  • Bug bounty, over Lightning, for confirmed bugs and useful reports - by severity:
    • Useful feedback: 500 sats (a clear note on what confused you, with the screen and the step)
    • Bug, low: 1,000 sats (a wrong text or a layout glitch that does not stop play)
    • Bug, medium: 2,000 sats (a round or a deposit that needs a retry to go through)
    • Bug, high: 5,000 sats (play or a payout stops and only a reload or a restart helps)
    • Bug, critical: 10,000 sats (money can be lost or taken, or the result is wrong)
  • Node bounty: 10,000 sats for running a node, for the first 10 outside nodes - one per person and one per node, reported from the node's owner page.
  • Never for game results. Nobody is paid for winning a round.
  • Test sats have no value. Only these Lightning payments are real money.

Found something? Use Feedback at the bottom of the game page.

How a report was paid

  • Checked first. Each report is read on its ticket and marked valid or not, with a severity: critical, high, medium or low.
  • Approved and paid by the project owner, who answers for every payout. If the owner sets a different severity, that is written on the ticket.
  • No limit per person. Every confirmed report is paid, however many one tester sends: in Week 1 one tester has 21 paid reports, another 15.
  • A weekly budget of 100,000 sats. Paid so far in Week 1 (Nov 11-13): 94,500 sats.
  • Follow-ups are paid less. A new finding gets its tier. A report that finds a gap in the fix for an earlier report, or another variant of a bug that is already known, is a follow-up: it is fixed like any other report, but paid 1,000 sats, because it builds on the earlier finding and on the fix made for it. In Week 1 that was 15 reports (10 high, 5 medium) at 1,000 sats; the first report of the federation-record chain (#037, high) was paid 2,000 sats.
  • Paid in Week 1, by tier: critical 10,000 (1 report), high 5,000 (8), medium 2,000 (7), low 1,000 (6), useful 500 (3), and the follow-ups above. Node bounty: none paid yet.
  • From one bounty wallet, over Lightning, kept only for this. Every payout is listed below with its report.
  • The network's reserves never pay a single report. They may top up the bounty wallet once a year, by a public proposal and vote.
