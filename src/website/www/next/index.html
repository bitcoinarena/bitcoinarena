A round has four steps, from deposit to payout

DepositPay a Lightning invoice. The sats arrive as federation e-cash in your browser wallet.
StakeJoin a round. Your stake is locked in the federation.
VerifyThe round’s nodes replay it. 5 of 7 must sign the same result.
WinWinnings land in your wallet. Withdraw over Lightning.

Your stake is locked in a federation, not held by us

It leaves the lock one of two ways: 5 of 7 nodes sign the result, or the time runs out and it comes back to you.
