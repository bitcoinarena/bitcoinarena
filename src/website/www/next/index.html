Games and prediction markets for bitcoin, run by independent nodes

Many independent nodes run Bitcoin Arena. No company owns it. A federation of those nodes holds the sats, and no single node can move them. The nodes check every result before anyone is paid. You deposit and withdraw over Lightning in seconds, and your Nostr key is your login. The code is open: read it, or run your own node.

A round has four steps, from deposit to payout

DepositPay a Lightning invoice. Your sats arrive in the browser wallet as e-cash of a federation.
StakeJoin a round. Your stake is locked in the federation, not paid to an operator.
VerifyThe round’s committee of nodes replays the runs or checks the signed price. 5 of 7 must sign the same result.
PaidWinnings land back in your wallet. Withdraw to any Lightning wallet.

Your stake is locked in a federation, not held by us

Each claim below, and where you can check it. Every node answers the same paths.

ClaimWhat backs it
No central server. Rounds run on independent nodes. Any operator with a bond can host them; no company runs the game. Each node’s bond on Bitcoin testnet4. A node serves its bond with the proof into its block at GET /bond. The node table further down lists them all.
No single custodian. Your stake is locked in a federation of independent nodes, not paid to an operator. Taking it would need most of them acting together. The federations, their guardians and how many must sign: GET /federations and GET /reserves. Whether a node has seen your stake in its lock: GET /settlement?entry=…
5 of 7 seats sign the same result. The nodes replay every run. A result counts only when 5 of the round’s 7 seats sign the same one. Each seat’s signed result: GET /results?round=…&entry=…. The run itself, replayed in your browser: GET /replay?round=…&entry=…. Who sits on the round: GET /committee?round=…
Anyone can object for 10 minutes after a result. POST /objection at any node of the round. The window is 600,000 ms in the round’s terms (objectionWindowMs).
The nodes are found on Bitcoin. Every node posts a bond and a signed advertisement on Bitcoin. Your browser finds the nodes there and checks them itself. This page does it: the node table below proves each bond into its block and checks each signature in your browser (js/chain-nodes.js).

No single person, company or node can take a player’s money. From version 2.0, the guardians’ bonds stand behind the bitcoin a federation holds. The security page goes through every worst case: a hacked node, guardians who steal, a federation that stops, a lost phone. For each, what stops it and what is left.

Your Nostr key is your account

No sign-up, no email, no password. Log in to play with your Nostr key, restore your wallet from it on any device, pay from your own wallet over Nostr Wallet Connect, and run a node with the same key.

Log in with NostrAn nsec, a NIP-07 browser extension, or a NIP-46 remote signer: a bunker link or a QR code for your phone signer.
Your wallet follows youYour game wallet’s seed is kept on Nostr, NIP-44 encrypted to your own key (NIP-78). Log in anywhere and it is back.
Nostr Wallet ConnectConnect your Lightning wallet once (NIP-47) to pay deposits and stakes, and to withdraw winnings into it.
Run a node with your keyNode owners claim and manage their node with a Nostr key. With an extension or remote signer, every owner action is a signed NIP-98 event (kind 27235) that the node checks.
The Arena relayrelay.bitcoin-arena.io keeps profiles, relay lists and encrypted seeds, and carries remote signing and wallet requests. Nothing else.
Community on NostrThe Bitcoin Arena community is on Armada, a Nostr chat app. Join with the same key.

90 % of every stake is in play

The split of a paid stake is fixed. It is the same in every round and every game.

ShareGoes to
90 %In playThe pot the round pays out.
4 %Node feeThe nodes that verify the round and hold the money.
2 %Game developerThe game’s developer.
2 %Protocol developersThe protocol developers.
2 %Protocol reservesThe network’s own fund, below.

An item pays 40 % to the nodes and 20 % to the protocol reserves, plus the developers’ share you choose.

2 % of every paid round and 20 % of every item go to the protocol reserves, locked to each federation’s 5-of-7 key. Anyone can propose a payout. It is paid only if the bonded nodes vote for it. Read and discuss the proposals →

Bonds, committees and payouts all rest on Bitcoin

BondsEvery node posts a bond on Bitcoin. No bond, no seat.
CommitteesEach round’s committee is drawn from the bonded nodes by a Bitcoin block hash.
ReplayGames are deterministic. Nodes re-run every input and sign the result.
CustodyStakes lock in a Fedimint federation whose guardians are the nodes, never in one company’s wallet.
SettlementPayouts and withdrawals move as sats over Lightning.
