Where is your stake

When you join a round, your stake goes into a lock held by a federation of seven guardians. Not by us, and not by the other player.

It leaves the lock one of two ways. Five of the seven nodes sign the same result, and the winners are paid from the losers' locks. Or the round's time runs out, and the lock pays you back.

Every result is a replay of every move, so any node, or you, can check it.

From the developer

6 November 2026

What runs: three games on Bitcoin testnet4, with real nodes, real bonds and two federations holding the stakes. Every result is signed by five of seven nodes and can be replayed by anyone. What is missing: mainnet, games from other developers, and a lot of polish on the first minute of play.

What broke last: this week I went through every place in the code where a stake, a payout or a signature changes state, and found 47 where a crash, an unsigned request or one node's word could move money the wrong way. The critical ones are being fixed and tested first. Before that, a test showed that three silent nodes can hold a round until its locks run out: the loser gets the stake back and the winner goes unpaid. That one is open, and it needs a rule, not a patch.

If something breaks for you, tell me. That is what the beta is for.
