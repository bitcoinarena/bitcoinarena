Privacy

We count how many people use the site and the games, so we know what works. We do not know who you are.

What we measure

We run our own copy of Umami, open-source analytics. It sets no cookies. It counts:

  • Page visits, the page, the referring site, and browser and country in general terms
  • Tagged links such as utm_source, to see which post brought a visit
  • How far a page is scrolled, which of its sections are reached, and which downloads are clicked
  • Steps in the app: wallet created, login, deposit, withdrawal, round joined, round finished
  • Whether a step came from a Nostr login or a normal wallet

What we never collect

  • Keys, seeds, npubs or wallet data
  • Amounts, round ids or node addresses
  • Your name or email: there is no sign-up

What is public anyway

Game results are public by design. Nodes sign them, and anyone can read them under the round key or the name you play as. The number of weekly testers comes from these signed results, so anyone can check it.
